Gamepads work too. On a phone, turn it sideways and use the on-screen buttons.

The rules

  • Volts: 100 earn an extra life.
  • Neon Shards: three per level, often off the main path. They count toward your rank.
  • Arcade Tokens: one per level, hidden in a secret room. Find all six to unlock Retro Vee.
  • Pulse Shield: blocks the next hit.
  • Overdrive Core: faster running and a double jump for 12 seconds.
  • ? blocks: hit them from below.
  • Jump on rounded enemies. Never jump on red, spiky ones.
  • Some walls are hollow. If a gap looks suspicious, walk into it.
