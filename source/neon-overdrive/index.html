    Drift to charge. Hold Shift while you steer at speed. Sliding, and driving cleanly, fill the reserve gauge under the speed.

    Boost spends it. Hold B on straights and to overtake. The gauge drains fast.

    Cut the Spine. The lit channel down the middle is shorter and faster. Its gate only opens if your reserve is past the amber mark as you reach it; a sealed gate throws you out and costs most of your speed.

    Stay off the barriers. The shoulder slows you and walls hurt. Three laps, four checkpoints a lap.
