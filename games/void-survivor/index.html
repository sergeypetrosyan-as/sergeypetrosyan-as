    How to play

    • Move with WASD or the arrow keys (touch: drag on the left). Wick fires on its own.
    • Collect Essence motes to level up, then slot one of three modules (keys 1 to 3, 4 rerolls).
    • Siphon turns kills into a Reserve. Bulwark Field spends it as a dome that eats bolts and burns enemies.
    • Detonation fires when the Field breaks or when you press E or Space. Press Q to hold the Reserve and charge a bigger blast.
    • Read the floor: pink lines are shots, amber chevrons are charges, striped lanes and violet rings are hazards. Flank the cyan shield plates.
    • Survive 20 minutes or bring down the Final Warden. Shards you earn buy permanent salvage in the Vault.
