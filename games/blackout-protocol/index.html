        Rules of the dark

        • Guards see you only inside their flashlight cone, with a clear line of sight. Walls, lockers and closed doors block it. Cameras work the same way.
        • Unlit floor (tread plate, no ceiling light) cuts sight range to 40 percent; sneaking cuts it by another quarter. Lockers hide you completely, unless a guard sees you climb in.
        • The bar over a guard fills while it sees you; the white tick is where it turns suspicious. Triangle with a question mark: suspicious. Diamond with an exclamation mark: alert and chasing. Ring with three dots: searching for 15 seconds.
        • The ring at your feet shows how visible you are: solid with rays when you stand in light, dashed when you are in the dark.
        • Energy powers hacks: loop a camera (35), lock or unlock a door (15), drop a decoy (20), cut a laser grid (30). It refills after a second without hacking.
        • Terminals ask you to repeat a sequence from memory. A wrong key locks the terminal and raises an alarm. Collect every objective, then reach the extraction plate.
